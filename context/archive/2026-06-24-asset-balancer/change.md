## Notes

Roadmap slice S-15 (`asset-balancer`). A new "Balance" page where the user picks an
investment set from their existing assets, sets a target % per asset (share of a
configurable investment pool, NOT of total net worth), and compares declared vs real
allocation as two pie charts. Plus a per-asset "% of all assets" label on the assets list.
See `context/foundation/roadmap.md` → S-15 for prerequisites, unknowns, and risk notes.
