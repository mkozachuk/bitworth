# Net Worth Tracker — MVP Idea

## What is it?

A personal web app to track your total financial wealth across different asset types and currencies. Instead of maintaining a monthly spreadsheet, you open the app, enter your current balances, and instantly see your full financial picture converted into a single currency — with automatic comparison to last month and the beginning of the year.

The core idea is simple: you know what you own, but it’s scattered across cash in different currencies, investment funds, bonds, and crypto. This app brings it all together in one number, updated monthly, with a clear view of how that number is changing over time.

## Who is it for?

Anyone who manually tracks their personal finances in a spreadsheet and wants a cleaner, more visual way to do it — without connecting bank accounts or using complex financial tools.

-----

## What’s in the MVP

- **Asset input** — manually enter balances across categories: cash (PLN, USD, EUR), investment funds, bonds, crypto
- **Live currency conversion** — all assets are automatically converted to a single display currency using real exchange rates and crypto prices
- **Total net worth** — one clear number showing your overall wealth
- **Monthly snapshot** — the app saves your data once a month so history is preserved automatically
- **Delta indicators** — see how your net worth changed vs. last month and vs. the start of the year
- **Net worth chart** — a simple line graph showing your wealth over time across saved snapshots

-----

## What’s NOT in the MVP

- Connecting to banks or brokers — all input is manual
- Multiple user accounts or sharing
- Budgeting or expense tracking
- Goal setting
- Notifications or reminders
- Currency display switcher
- PDF or CSV export
- Inflation-adjusted calculations
- Mobile app
